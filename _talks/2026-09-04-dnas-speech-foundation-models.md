Invited talk at the Division of Natural and Applied Sciences (DNAs) Seminar Series, Duke Kunshan University.

Pre-trained speech foundation models provide broadly transferable representations, but downstream adaptation raises two complementary questions: what should be learned during post-training, and where should task-specific capacity be introduced? This talk presents a unified view of efficient and reliable post-training for speech foundation models, covering supervised fine-tuning, reward-guided post-training, dynamic prompt adaptation, mixture-of-experts adapters, and spectral low-rank adaptation. The discussion emphasizes parameter-efficient methods that improve task specialization and generalization while retaining the strengths of pre-trained speech representations.
