## 2026

### [Speech Large Language Models for Under-Resourced Languages](https://interspeech2026.org/en-AU/pages/programme/tutorials)

*Interspeech 2026*, September 27, 2026, Sydney, Australia.

**Instructors:** Zhe Li (HKU), Mieradilijiang Maimaiti (Xinjiang University), Hao Huang (Xinjiang University), Nurmemet Yolwas (Xinjiang University), Ricky Chan (HKU).

This tutorial introduces Speech Large Language Models for under-resourced languages, with an emphasis on linguistic diversity, data scarcity, and multilingual transfer. It traces the progression from cascaded ASR, NLP, and TTS pipelines to multilingual speech foundation models and Speech LLMs, including tokenization, unified modeling, and vocoders. It also covers multilingual pre-training, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, post-training alignment, and open challenges for low-resource speech.

### [Speech Large Language Models: Architectures, Efficient Adaptation, and Applications](https://2026.ieeeicme.org/tutorials/#1766933845252-bb9d3b7e-7e8e)

*IEEE International Conference on Multimedia and Expo (ICME 2026)*, July 5–9, 2026, Bangkok, Thailand.

**Instructors:** Zhe Li (HKU), Zezhong Jin (PolyU), Chong-Xin Gan (PolyU), Mert Pilanci (Stanford).

This tutorial presents the transition from cascaded ASR–LLM–TTS pipelines to unified Speech LLMs, including AudioPaLM and SpeechGPT, and considers their latency and paralinguistic benefits. It examines semantic and acoustic tokenizers, language models, interleaved speech–text modeling, and parameter-efficient adaptation with LoRA and adapters. The tutorial also discusses post-training alignment with SFT, DPO, and GRPO, alongside evaluation for speech understanding, generation, and full-duplex interaction.
